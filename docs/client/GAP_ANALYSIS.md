Every requirement that is not VERIFIED COMPLETE or NOT APPLICABLE in the matrix appears below. Complexity is a relative engineering estimate (Low / Medium / High), not a schedule. Items that depend on a client decision cannot start until it is answered.

**Priority key:** **P0** payment correctness, membership lifecycle, security, inventory/order/data integrity — must be closed before production. **P1** loyalty, referrals, shipping, exchanges, refunds, notifications, invoices, admin workflows — needed for V1 acceptance. **P2** reporting, analytics, UI refinement, automation, future integrations.
